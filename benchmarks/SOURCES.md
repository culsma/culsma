# Sources and provenance

The case text and programs accompany the paper. Cases 01–07 and 14 synthesize
established laboratory procedures; the references identify representative
methods rather than verbatim source protocols. Cases 08–09 adapt versioned
public protocols. Cases 10–13 derive from specified sections of one manual.
Case 00 is the worked example and is excluded from corpus totals.
