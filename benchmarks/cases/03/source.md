# Full Western blot workflow

## Workflow

**Cell Harvest, Wash, and Protein Lysis**

1. Culture 5 x 10^6 adherent mammalian cells in one well of a 6-well plate until the protein harvest point.
2. Aspirate and discard the culture medium without disturbing the adherent cell monolayer.
3. Wash the adherent monolayer twice with 1 mL 1x PBS per wash. For each wash, add PBS, gently rinse the monolayer, then aspirate and discard the PBS without disturbing the cells.
4. Add 300 uL cold RIPA lysis buffer containing protease and phosphatase inhibitors directly to the washed monolayer.
5. Lyse the cells on the plate on ice for 20 minutes.
6. Transfer the crude lysate from the well to a clean microcentrifuge tube.
7. Pipette the crude lysate 10 times using 240 uL per mix, until no visible clumps remain.
8. Centrifuge the crude lysate at 12,000 x g for 10 minutes at 4 C.
9. Transfer the clarified supernatant to a clean microcentrifuge tube and keep it on ice.
10. Discard the insoluble pellet.

**Protein Quantification**

11. Prepare BCA working reagent by mixing BCA reagent A and BCA reagent B at 50:1.
12. Prepare five BSA standards at 0, 125, 500, 1000, and 2000 ug/mL.
13. Add 10 uL clarified lysate to each sample well of a 96-well assay plate in duplicate.
14. Add 10 uL blank or BSA standard to each standard well in duplicate.
15. Add 200 uL BCA working reagent to each assay, blank, and standard well.
16. Incubate the BCA plate at 37 C for 30 minutes.
17. Measure absorbance at 590 nm.
18. Calculate the protein concentration of the clarified lysate from the BSA standard curve.
19. Determine the volume of clarified lysate required to load 30 ug total protein per SDS-PAGE lane.

**Reducing Sample Preparation**

20. Prepare reducing 4x protein loading buffer by adding reducing agent to a final 10% v/v in the 4x loading buffer.
21. Combine clarified lysate containing 30 ug total protein with reducing 4x loading buffer and PBS to a final 1x loading buffer concentration in a 25 uL prepared sample.
22. Mix the prepared sample by pipetting 10 times using 20 uL per mix.
23. Heat the prepared sample at 98 C for 15 minutes to denature proteins.
24. Briefly centrifuge the denatured sample at 3000 x g for 5 seconds to collect condensate at the bottom of the tube.

**SDS-PAGE**

25. Place a 4-12% Bis-Tris SDS-PAGE gel into the electrophoresis tank.
26. Fill the electrophoresis tank with 800 mL 1x SDS-PAGE running buffer.
27. Load 25 uL prepared protein sample into the sample lane.
28. Load 10 uL prestained molecular weight marker into the marker lane.
29. Run the gel at 100 V for 15 minutes to move samples through the stacking region.
30. Increase the voltage to 150 V and continue electrophoresis for 45 minutes, or until the dye front reaches the lower region of the gel.

**Protein Transfer**

31. Activate the PVDF membrane in methanol for 1 minute, then aspirate and discard the methanol.
32. Rinse the activated PVDF membrane in deionized water for 2 minutes, then aspirate and discard the water.
33. Equilibrate the gel and PVDF membrane in transfer buffer containing 20% methanol for 15 minutes.
34. Aspirate and discard the PVDF equilibration buffer before assembling the transfer stack.
35. Place the gel and equilibrated PVDF membrane in the transfer stack with no visible air bubbles between gel and membrane.
36. Add transfer buffer to the transfer stack.
37. Transfer proteins from the gel to the PVDF membrane at 100 V for 90 minutes at 4 C.
38. Move the transferred membrane to a fresh membrane-processing tank for blocking.

**Blocking and Primary Antibody Incubation**

39. Prepare blocking buffer as 5% nonfat milk in 1x TBST.
40. Rinse the transferred membrane once with 15 mL 1x TBST and aspirate the TBST.
41. Incubate the membrane in 15 mL blocking buffer for 1 hour at 25 C on a platform shaker at 60 rpm.
42. Wash the membrane 3 times with 15 mL 1x TBST for 5 minutes per wash at 25 C on a platform shaker at 60 rpm.
43. Prepare primary antibody dilution buffer as 5% BSA in 1x TBST.
44. Dilute mouse anti-alpha-tubulin primary antibody clone DM1A at 1:1000 in 10 mL primary antibody dilution buffer.
45. Incubate the membrane with the diluted primary antibody overnight at 4 C on a platform shaker at 60 rpm.
46. Wash the membrane 3 times with 15 mL 1x TBST for 5 minutes per wash at 25 C on a platform shaker at 60 rpm.

**Secondary Antibody Incubation and Imaging**

47. Dilute goat anti-mouse IgG Alexa Fluor 680 secondary antibody at 1:10000 in 10 mL blocking buffer.
48. Incubate the membrane with the diluted secondary antibody for 1 hour at 25 C on a platform shaker at 60 rpm, protected from light.
49. Wash the membrane 3 times with 15 mL 1x TBST for 5 minutes per wash at 25 C on a platform shaker at 60 rpm, protected from light.
50. Remove excess wash buffer without allowing the membrane to dry.
51. Acquire a Western blot image in the 680 nm fluorescence channel for alpha-tubulin.
52. Return the antibody-stained membrane and associated Western blot image record as the protocol outputs.
