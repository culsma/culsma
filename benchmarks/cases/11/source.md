### 2.2 cDNA Template Switch

This standalone benchmark case starts with bead-bound captured cDNA from the preceding capture workflow, represented here as the RV02 input material. After an additional wash, the Template Switch Master Mix is added to the captured cDNA. The template switch reaction adds a 5-prime adapter to the cDNA.

#### Reagents

| Item | Source | Quantity | Handling and storage |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Wash Buffer 3 | -20C Reagents | 1 | Thaw and store at room temperature. Mix by inverting 3x. |
| Template Switch Buffer | -20C Reagents | 1 | Thaw at room temperature, then place on ice. Mix by inverting 3x. Briefly centrifuge before use. |
| Template Switch Primer | -20C Reagents | 1 | Thaw at room temperature, then place on ice. Mix by inverting 3x. Briefly centrifuge before use. |
| Template Switch Enzyme | -20C Reagents | 1 | Keep on ice. Briefly centrifuge before use. |

Ensure there is no precipitate in the Template Switch Buffer before proceeding.

#### Template Switch Master Mix

Prepare the Template Switch Master Mix in a new 2 mL tube. Mix by pipetting 10x and store on ice.

| Number of samples | Template Switch Buffer | Template Switch Primer | Template Switch Enzyme | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 101.75 uL | 2.75 uL | 5.5 uL | 110 uL |
| 16 | 1628 uL | 44 uL | 88 uL | 1760 uL |

#### Template Switch Procedure

1. Place each tube of bead-bound captured cDNA input material on the high position of the magnetic rack.
2. Incubate until the solution clears, about 2 minutes.
3. While still on the magnetic rack, remove and discard the supernatant.
4. While still on the magnetic rack, add 125 uL of Wash Buffer 3 to each tube. Do not discard the Wash Buffer 3 until directed, because it is used in another step.
5. Incubate for 1 minute at room temperature.
6. While still on the magnetic rack, remove and discard Wash Buffer 3.
7. Remove the tube(s) from the magnetic rack.
8. Fully resuspend each bead pellet with 100 uL of the Template Switch Master Mix. Because the mix is viscous, full resuspension may take time.
9. Briefly centrifuge without letting beads collect at the bottom of the tube(s).
10. Incubate for 30 minutes at room temperature.
11. Fully resuspend each bead pellet by mixing 5x with a P200 set to 75 uL.
12. Place the tube(s) into a thermocycler and run the Template Switch program.

| Run time | Lid temperature | Sample volume |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 60 min | 70C | 100 uL |

| Step | Time | Temperature |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 60 min | 42C |
| 2 | Hold | 4C |

13. Continue immediately to the cDNA amplification module, or proceed to the storage steps below before cDNA amplification.
14. For optional storage, place the tube(s) on the high position of the magnetic rack and incubate until the solution clears, about 2 minutes. Beads may need to be resuspended if settled.
15. While still on the magnetic rack, remove and discard the supernatant.
16. Remove the tube(s) from the magnetic rack.
17. Fully resuspend each bead pellet with 125 uL Wash Buffer 2.
18. Template-switched cDNA can be stored at 4C for up to 18 hours. Do not freeze.
