# Cell culture, transfection, immunocytochemistry, and imaging

## 1. Seed RPE1 Cells for Fixed-Cell Imaging

1. Use one well labeled A1 in an 8-well glass chamber slide as the representative fixed-cell imaging chamber.
2. Treat each chamber well as having a maximum working medium capacity of 800 uL.
3. Add 300 uL hTERT-RPE1 cell suspension to chamber A1.
4. Add 350 uL complete DMEM/F12 culture medium to chamber A1.
5. Incubate the chamber for 24 h at 37 C with 5% CO2.

## 2. Prepare and Apply Transfection Mixture

1. Prepare one transfection mixture tube.
2. Add 100 uL Opti-MEM to the transfection mixture tube.
3. Add 4 uL Lipofectamine 2000 to the transfection mixture tube.
4. Add 2 uL plasmid DNA to the transfection mixture tube.
5. Incubate the transfection mixture for 20 min at room temperature.
6. Add the full 106 uL transfection mixture to the RPE1 imaging well.
7. Incubate the transfected RPE1 cells for 48 h at 37 C with 5% CO2.

## 3. Serum-Starve Cells for the Representative Imaging Condition

1. Remove and discard the culture medium from the adherent RPE1 cells by aspiration while retaining the adherent cells.
2. Add 500 uL serum-free DMEM/F12 medium to the RPE1 imaging chamber.
3. Incubate the cells for 48 h at 37 C with 5% CO2.

## 4. Fix Cells

1. Remove and discard the serum-free medium by aspiration while retaining the adherent cells.
2. Add 300 uL 4% paraformaldehyde in PBS to the RPE1 imaging chamber.
3. Incubate the cells for 20 min at room temperature.
4. Remove and discard the fixation solution by aspiration while retaining the adherent cells.
5. Wash the fixed cells three times with PBS.
6. For each PBS wash, add 500 uL PBS to the chamber, remove and discard the PBS by aspiration, and retain the adherent cells.

## 5. Permeabilize, Quench, and Block Cells

1. Remove residual liquid from the fixed-cell chamber by aspiration while retaining the adherent cells.
2. Add 300 uL Triton X-100 extraction buffer to the chamber.
3. Incubate the cells for 10 min at room temperature.
4. Remove and discard the Triton extraction buffer by aspiration while retaining the adherent cells.
5. Add 300 uL sodium borohydride solution in PBS to the chamber.
6. Incubate the cells for 7 min at room temperature.
7. Remove and discard the sodium borohydride solution by aspiration while retaining the adherent cells.
8. Add 300 uL 5% BSA blocking buffer in PBS to the chamber.
9. Incubate the cells for 30 min at room temperature.

## 6. Primary Antibody Incubation and Washes

1. Remove and discard the blocking buffer by aspiration while retaining the adherent cells.
2. Add 300 uL primary antibody solution to the chamber.
3. Incubate the cells with primary antibody for 16 h at 4 C.
4. Remove and discard the primary antibody solution by aspiration while retaining the adherent cells.
5. Wash the cells three times with PBS.
6. For each post-primary wash, add 500 uL PBS to the chamber, remove and discard the PBS by aspiration, and retain the adherent cells.

## 7. Secondary Antibody Incubation and Washes

1. Remove residual post-primary wash liquid by aspiration while retaining the adherent cells.
2. Add 300 uL Alexa Fluor secondary antibody solution to the chamber.
3. Incubate the cells with secondary antibody for 1 h at room temperature.
4. Remove and discard the secondary antibody solution by aspiration while retaining the adherent cells.
5. Wash the cells three times with PBS.
6. For each post-secondary wash, add 500 uL PBS to the chamber, remove and discard the PBS by aspiration, and retain the adherent cells.

## 8. Nuclear Staining and Image Acquisition

1. Remove residual post-secondary wash liquid by aspiration while retaining the adherent cells.
2. Add 300 uL DAPI working nuclear stain to the chamber.
3. Incubate the cells with DAPI working nuclear stain for 15 min at room temperature.
4. Remove and discard the DAPI working stain by aspiration while retaining the adherent cells.
5. Wash the stained cells once by adding 500 uL PBS to the chamber.
6. Remove and discard the PBS wash by aspiration while retaining the adherent cells.
7. Add 100 uL mounting solution to the stained cell chamber.
8. Acquire a confocal cell image from the mounted RPE1 imaging chamber.
9. Record cell count, fluorescence signal, and field quality for the image readout.
