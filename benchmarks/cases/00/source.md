# GFP lysate clarification and fluorescence readout

## Context

This independently authored teaching example prepares a clarified lysate from
GFP-expressing microbial cells for a fluorescence readout. It is not reproduced
from a published protocol. The clarified lysate is retained and the debris pellet
is discarded.

## Steps

1. Prepare 100000 washed GFP-expressing microbial cells in 80 uL lysis-compatible resuspension buffer.

2. Prepare at least 80 uL 2x lysis buffer.

3. Prepare one 1.5 mL lysis tube.

4. Prepare one 1.0 mL clarified-lysate output tube.

5. Prepare one 1.0 mL debris-waste tube.

6. Transfer 80 uL washed GFP-expressing microbial-cell suspension containing 100000 cells and 80 uL 2x lysis buffer into the 1.5 mL lysis tube to produce a 1x final lysis condition.

7. Mix the lysis mixture by pipetting 10 times.

8. Incubate the lysis mixture at 37 C for 30 min.

9. Centrifuge the lysate at 12000 x g and 22 C for 10 min to resolve a clarified supernatant fraction and a debris-pellet fraction.

10. Transfer 100 uL clarified supernatant fraction to the clarified-lysate output tube.

11. Discard the debris-pellet fraction.

12. Record a fluorescence readout from the clarified lysate.

## Notes

The listed quantities and conditions define the default teaching example.
Fluorescence interpretation, normalization and acceptance thresholds require
separate analysis and are not specified by this procedure.
