# Whole-Blood LPS Stimulation, Supernatant Collection, and RBC Lysis

## Steps

1. Start with 100 uL whole blood in one well of a 2 mL deep-well plate.
2. Add 100 uL LPS stimulation medium containing 2 ng/mL LPS in HPLM with 10% FBS, giving a total volume of 200 uL and a final LPS concentration of 1 ng/mL.
3. Mix by pipetting 5 times with the pipette set to 160 uL.
4. Incubate at 37 C with 5% CO2 for 6 h.
5. Centrifuge at 2,000 x g for 10 min at 4 C.
6. Transfer the supernatant to a separate collection well.
7. Retain the cell pellet in the original well.
8. Add 1 mL eBioscience 1X RBC Lysis Buffer (00-4333-57) and pipette 5 times with the pipette set to 800 uL at 20 C.
9. Incubate at 20 C for 15 min.
10. Centrifuge at 500 x g for 5 min at 4 C.
11. Remove and discard the supernatant, retaining the cell pellet in the original well.
12. Add 1 mL FACS buffer and pipette 5 times with the pipette set to 800 uL at 20 C.
13. Centrifuge at 300 x g for 5 min at 4 C.
14. Remove and discard the supernatant, retaining the cell pellet in the original well.
15. Retain the stimulation supernatant and the washed cell pellet separately for downstream assays.
