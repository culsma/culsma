# PBMC HIA Stimulation, Antibody Staining, Fixation, and Flow-Cytometry Acquisition

## Steps

1. Prepare 10 mL staining buffer by combining 200 uL FBS with 9.8 mL 1x PBS and inverting 5 times.
2. Start with 1 million PBMCs in 1 mL of DMEM culture medium in a 15 mL culture vessel.
3. Add 5 uL anti-HIA antibody. 
4. Incubate for 24 h at 37 C and 5% CO2.
5. Centrifuge at 500 x g for 10 min at 20 C, then remove and discard the culture supernatant without disturbing the cell pellet.
6. Add 2 mL staining buffer (2% FBS in 1x PBS) and pipette 10 times using 1 mL per stroke to resuspend the cells.
7. Centrifuge at 300 x g for 5 min at 4 C, then remove and discard the supernatant without disturbing the cell pellet.
8. Add 65 uL staining buffer to the cell pellet and pipette 10 times using 50 uL per stroke to resuspend the cells into a single-cell suspension.
9. Add 5 uL BD Pharmingen Human BD Fc Block (BD Biosciences, catalog number 564220; stock concentration 0.5 mg/mL), corresponding to 2.5 ug per sample.
10. Pipette the 70 uL suspension 10 times using 50 uL per stroke, then incubate for 10 min at 20 C.
11. Prepare 20 uL antibody staining mix by combining 2 uL FITC anti-human CD45 (BioLegend 304054; clone HI30; stock concentration 0.5 mg/mL), 2 uL PE anti-human CD4 (BioLegend 300550; clone RPA-T4; stock concentration 0.2 mg/mL), 2 uL PerCP/Cyanine5.5 anti-human CD8 (BioLegend 344710; clone SK1; stock concentration 0.5 mg/mL), and 2 uL APC anti-human CD19 (BioLegend 302212; clone HIB19; stock concentration 0.5 mg/mL) with 12 uL staining buffer. Vortex for 5 s.
12. Add the complete 20 uL staining mix to the 70 uL cell suspension, giving a total volume of 90 uL.
13. Pipette the 90 uL staining suspension 10 times using 80 uL per stroke, then incubate for 30 min on ice protected from light.
14. Add 2 mL staining buffer. Centrifuge at 300 x g for 5 min at 4 C, then remove and discard the supernatant without disturbing the cell pellet.
15. Add 2 mL fresh staining buffer and pipette 10 times using 1 mL per stroke to fully resuspend the cells.
16. Centrifuge at 300 x g for 5 min at 4 C, then remove and discard the supernatant without disturbing the cell pellet.
17. Prepare 300 uL 1x fixation buffer by combining 30 uL 10x fixation-buffer stock with 270 uL PBS. Add the complete 300 uL to the cell pellet.
18. Pipette the 300 uL suspension 10 times using 240 uL per stroke, then incubate for 10 min at 22 C protected from light.
19. Add 2 mL staining buffer. Centrifuge at 300 x g for 5 min at 4 C, then remove and discard the supernatant without disturbing the cell pellet.
20. Add 500 uL staining buffer and pipette 10 times using 400 uL per stroke to fully resuspend the fixed cells.
21. Acquire the sample by flow cytometry and record FSC-A, FSC-H, SSC-A, and the fluorescence signals for CD45-FITC, CD4-PE, CD8-PerCP/Cyanine5.5, and CD19-APC. Save the raw event data with the sample identifier, event identifiers, acquisition configuration identifier, and acquisition quality status as the final readout.
