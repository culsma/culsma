# Module 05: Magnetic-Bead Immunoprecipitation

## Steps

1. Start with 500 uL clarified protein lysate containing 1 mg total protein in IP lysis buffer.
2. Transfer 25 uL clarified lysate to a clean 1.5 mL tube as the input fraction and keep it at 0 C until the immunoprecipitation is complete.
3. Vortex Protein G magnetic-bead slurry for 30 s to obtain a homogeneous suspension.
4. Transfer 50 uL homogeneous Protein G magnetic-bead slurry to a clean 1.5 mL tube.
5. Place the bead tube on a magnetic rack for 2 min at 22 C, then remove and discard the storage supernatant while retaining the magnetic beads.
6. Prepare 200 uL capture-antibody solution containing 5 ug anti-GFP antibody in antibody binding and washing buffer, then add the complete solution to the retained beads.
7. Rotate the bead-antibody suspension at 20 rpm for 10 min at 22 C.
8. Place the tube on the magnetic rack for 2 min at 22 C, then remove and discard the supernatant while retaining the antibody-conjugated beads.
9. Add 200 uL antibody binding and washing buffer to the antibody-conjugated beads and pipette 10 times using 160 uL per stroke.
10. Place the tube on the magnetic rack for 2 min at 22 C, then remove and discard the wash supernatant while retaining the antibody-conjugated beads.
11. Add the remaining 475 uL clarified lysate and 25 uL antibody binding and washing buffer to the antibody-conjugated beads.
12. Rotate the 500 uL immunoprecipitation reaction at 20 rpm for 2 h at 4 C.
13. Place the tube on the magnetic rack for 2 min at 4 C.
14. Remove and discard the complete flowthrough without disturbing the retained beads.
15. Add 200 uL ice-cold IP wash buffer to the retained beads and pipette 10 times using 160 uL per stroke.
16. Rotate the bead suspension at 20 rpm for 5 min at 4 C.
17. Place the tube on the magnetic rack for 2 min at 4 C, then remove and discard the wash supernatant while retaining the beads.
18. Repeat steps 15-17 two additional times for a total of three washes.
19. Add 20 uL denaturing elution buffer and 10 uL premixed 4x LDS sample buffer containing reducing agent to the washed beads.
20. Pipette the 30 uL bead suspension 10 times using 24 uL per stroke, then heat it for 10 min at 70 C.
21. Place the tube on the magnetic rack for 2 min at 22 C.
22. Transfer the complete 30 uL eluate to a clean tube while leaving the spent magnetic beads in the original tube.
23. Return the 25 uL input fraction and 30 uL IP eluate as two separate materials while preserving their fraction identities for downstream analysis.
