# Module 01: Cell Transfection Using Cas9 RNP

1. Seed HEK293T-GENE-X-positive cells into a 24-well plate as three groups: WT control in well A1, non-targeting control in well A2, and deletion-RNP treatment in well A3.
2. Use 1.0 x 10^5 cells in 1 mL DMEM supplemented with 10% (v/v) FBS per well.
3. Culture the cells in the 24-well plate for 72 h at 37 C with 5% CO2.
4. Prepare one 10 uL deletion RNP reaction by combining 2 uL Cas9 protein stock at approximately 0.5 ug/uL, 2 uL sgRNA-1 stock at 0.5 ug/uL, 2 uL sgRNA-2 stock at 0.5 ug/uL, and 4 uL RNP assembly buffer.
5. Prepare one 10 uL non-targeting RNP reaction by combining 2 uL Cas9 protein stock at approximately 0.5 ug/uL, 4 uL non-targeting sgRNA stock at 0.5 ug/uL, and 4 uL RNP assembly buffer.
6. Incubate the deletion RNP and non-targeting RNP reactions for 10 min at room temperature.
7. Dilute 3.6 uL CRISPRMAX lipid transfection reagent in 60 uL Opti-MEM I medium, then combine with the full 10 uL deletion RNP reaction from step 4 to prepare the deletion delivery mixture.
8. Separately dilute 3.6 uL CRISPRMAX lipid transfection reagent in 60 uL Opti-MEM I medium, then combine with the full 10 uL non-targeting RNP reaction from step 5 to prepare the non-targeting delivery mixture.
9. Incubate both delivery mixtures for 15 min at room temperature.
10. Add the full non-targeting delivery mixture from step 8 dropwise to the non-targeting control well A2.
11. Add the full deletion delivery mixture from step 7 dropwise to the deletion-RNP treatment well A3.
12. Incubate the WT control, non-targeting control, and deletion-RNP-treated cells for 72 h at 37 C with 5% CO2.
13. Return the WT control cells in A1, non-targeting-RNP-treated cells in A2, and deletion-RNP-treated cells in A3, retaining their original well identities for downstream use. Editing outcomes remain to be determined by downstream assays.
