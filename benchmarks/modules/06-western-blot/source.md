# Module 06: Western Blot

## Steps

1. Start with 25 uL prepared protein sample containing 30 ug total protein in 1x reducing protein loading buffer.
2. Mix the prepared protein sample by pipetting 10 times using 20 uL per stroke.
3. Heat the prepared protein sample at 98 C for 15 min.
4. Spin down the sample at 3,000 x g for 5 s at 25 C.
5. Install one 4-12% Bis-Tris SDS-PAGE gel in an electrophoresis tank.
6. Add 800 mL 1x SDS-PAGE running buffer to the electrophoresis tank.
7. Load 25 uL denatured protein sample into the sample lane.
8. Load 10 uL prestained molecular-weight protein ladder into an adjacent marker lane.
9. Run the gel at 180 V for 30 min.
10. Add 15 mL deionized water and rinse the PVDF membrane for 2 min at 25 C.
11. Remove the water, add 15 mL transfer buffer containing 20% methanol, and equilibrate the membrane for 15 min at 25 C.
12. Equilibrate the separated gel in a separate 15 mL portion of transfer buffer containing 20% methanol for 15 min at 25 C.
13. Remove the membrane and gel equilibration buffers.
14. Assemble the transfer stack with the gel facing the negative electrode and the PVDF membrane facing the positive electrode, with no visible air bubbles between the gel and membrane.
15. Add 30 mL transfer buffer containing 20% methanol to keep the transfer stack wet.
16. Transfer proteins from the gel to the PVDF membrane at 100 V for 90 min at 4 C.
17. Move the transferred PVDF membrane to a clean membrane-processing tank.
18. Rinse the transferred membrane once with 15 mL 1x TBST, then remove the TBST.
19. Prepare 15 mL blocking buffer containing 5% w/v nonfat milk in 1x TBST.
20. Add the complete 15 mL blocking buffer to the membrane and shake at 60 rpm for 1 h at 25 C.
21. Remove and discard the blocking buffer.
22. Wash the membrane three times with 15 mL 1x TBST for 5 min per wash at 25 C with shaking at 60 rpm, removing the wash buffer after each wash.
23. Prepare 10 mL primary-antibody dilution buffer containing 5% w/v BSA in 1x TBST.
24. Prepare 10 mL primary-antibody solution containing mouse anti-alpha-tubulin clone DM1A diluted 1:1,000 in the primary-antibody dilution buffer.
25. Add the complete primary-antibody solution to the membrane and shake at 60 rpm for 18 h at 4 C.
26. Remove and discard the primary-antibody solution.
27. Wash the membrane three times with 15 mL 1x TBST for 5 min per wash at 25 C with shaking at 60 rpm, removing the wash buffer after each wash.
28. Prepare 10 mL secondary-antibody solution containing goat anti-mouse IgG Alexa Fluor 680 diluted 1:10,000 in blocking buffer.
29. Add the complete secondary-antibody solution to the membrane and shake at 60 rpm for 1 h at 25 C.
30. Remove and discard the secondary-antibody solution.
31. Wash the membrane three times with 15 mL 1x TBST for 5 min per wash at 25 C with shaking at 60 rpm, removing the wash buffer after each wash.
32. Acquire a Western blot image in the 680 nm fluorescence channel, including both the alpha-tubulin sample lane and the prestained protein-ladder lane.
