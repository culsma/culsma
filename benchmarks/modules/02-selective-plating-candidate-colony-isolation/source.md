# Module 02: Selective Plating and Candidate Colony Isolation

## Steps

1. Use 100 uL of each of five recovered DH5alpha transformation suspensions: recombinant ligation, vector-only ligation control, no-ligase vector control, uncut pUC19 positive transformation control, and no-DNA control.
2. Use five pre-poured 90 mm selective plates. Each plate contains 25 mL LB agar supplemented with 100 ug/mL ampicillin, 40 ug/mL X-gal, and 50 ug/mL IPTG.
3. Label one selective plate for each transformation suspension.
4. Transfer 100 uL of each recovered transformation suspension onto its corresponding selective plate.
5. Spread each suspension evenly across the agar surface using a sterile spreading tool.
6. Incubate the selective plates at 37 C for 16 h.
7. Inspect each plate manually. Record the plate identity and transformation group, then count the total, white, and blue colonies on the plate.
8. On the recombinant-ligation plate, identify white colonies that are well isolated and do not touch neighboring colonies. Assign each candidate colony a unique identifier and select eight colonies for expansion.
9. Use eight culture tubes, each preloaded with 4 mL LB broth supplemented with 100 ug/mL ampicillin.
10. Pick each selected white colony with a separate sterile pipette tip and transfer one complete colony into its assigned culture tube while preserving its `colony_id`.
11. Incubate the eight candidate-colony cultures at 37 C for 16 h with shaking at 200 rpm.
12. Return the eight candidate-colony cultures with their colony identifiers for downstream screening. The cultures are candidates and are not considered confirmed recombinant clones.
