# Recombinant Protein Affinity Purification With SDS-PAGE QC

## Protein Purification

1. Start with a 400 mg wet-weight bacterial expression cell pellet containing a His-tagged recombinant target protein. For benchmark material accounting, track 1 mg of the target protein within the starting pellet.
2. Resuspend the cell pellet in 2 mL ice-cold native lysis and binding buffer.
3. Mechanically lyse the resuspended cells on ice for 5 min.
4. Centrifuge the lysate at 12000 x g for 15 min at 4°C.
5. Transfer 1.62 mL clarified lysate to a new tube without disturbing the insoluble cell debris.
6. Save 20 uL clarified lysate as the input QC sample.
7. Equilibrate 400 uL nickel affinity resin in a gravity-flow column with 1 mL native binding buffer.
8. Let the binding buffer pass through the column by gravity and discard the equilibration flowthrough.
9. Apply the remaining 1.58 mL clarified lysate to the equilibrated nickel resin.
10. Incubate the lysate with the resin for 30 min at 4°C with gentle mixing to promote binding.
11. Let the column drain by gravity and collect the flowthrough fraction.
12. Save 20 uL flowthrough fraction as the flowthrough QC sample.
13. Add 1 mL native wash buffer containing 20-40 mM imidazole to the column.
14. Let the wash buffer pass through the column by gravity and collect the wash fraction.
15. Repeat the wash two more times for a total of three 1 mL washes.
16. Pool the wash fractions and save 20 uL pooled wash as the wash QC sample.
17. Add 500 uL native elution buffer containing 250-300 mM imidazole to the column.
18. Incubate the resin with elution buffer for 5 min at room temperature.
19. Let the elution buffer pass through the column by gravity and collect elution fraction 1.
20. Save 20 uL elution fraction 1 as the elution 1 QC sample.
21. Repeat elution once more with 500 uL native elution buffer.
22. Collect elution fraction 2.
23. Save 20 uL elution fraction 2 as the elution 2 QC sample.
24. Keep the flowthrough, wash, and elution fractions at 4°C until SDS-PAGE QC.

## SDS-PAGE QC

25. Prepare QC samples for the input, flowthrough, pooled wash, elution fraction 1, and elution fraction 2.
26. For each QC sample, mix 15 uL fraction with 15 uL 2x reducing SDS-PAGE loading dye.
27. Prepare one molecular weight ladder lane.
28. Heat the prepared QC samples at 95°C for 5 min.
29. Briefly centrifuge the heated QC samples to collect condensation and insoluble debris.
30. Load 20 uL prepared input QC sample into lane 1.
31. Load 20 uL prepared flowthrough QC sample into lane 2.
32. Load 20 uL prepared pooled wash QC sample into lane 3.
33. Load 20 uL prepared elution fraction 1 QC sample into lane 4.
34. Load 20 uL prepared elution fraction 2 QC sample into lane 5.
35. Load 10 uL molecular weight ladder into lane 6.
36. Run the SDS-PAGE gel at 200 V for 45 min in 1x SDS running buffer.
37. Remove the gel from the cassette.
38. Rinse the gel with water.
39. Stain the gel with Coomassie protein stain for 1 h with gentle shaking.
40. Destain the gel in water until the background is clear enough to evaluate bands.
41. Image the gel.
42. Record lane identity, expected target molecular weight, target band intensity, contaminant bands, and qualitative purity for each fraction.
43. Return the purified elution fractions and the SDS-PAGE QC readout.
