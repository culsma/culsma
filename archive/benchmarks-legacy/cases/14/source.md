# Magnetic Bead Immunoprecipitation with Western Blot Readout

## Prepare Antibody-Conjugated Magnetic Beads

1. Prepare 500 uL clarified cell lysate containing 1 mg total protein in IP lysis buffer and keep it on ice. For benchmark material accounting, define the target protein as 0.1 mg and the background proteins as 0.9 mg.
2. Transfer 25 uL clarified lysate to a clean tube as the 5% input fraction. Keep the input fraction on ice.
3. Vortex Protein G magnetic beads for 30 s to obtain a homogeneous suspension.
4. Transfer 50 uL Protein G magnetic-bead slurry to a 1.5 mL tube.
5. Place the tube on a magnetic rack for 2 min at 25 C and discard the storage supernatant.
6. Add 5 ug anti-GFP antibody diluted in 200 uL antibody binding and washing buffer.
7. Rotate the bead-antibody suspension for 10 min at 25 C.
8. Place the tube on the magnetic rack for 2 min and discard the supernatant.
9. Add 200 uL antibody binding and washing buffer and resuspend the beads by pipetting 10 times with a pipette set to 160 uL.
10. Place the tube on the magnetic rack for 2 min and discard the wash supernatant while retaining the antibody-conjugated beads.

## Immunoprecipitate the Target Protein

1. Add the remaining 475 uL clarified lysate and 25 uL antibody binding and washing buffer to the antibody-conjugated beads.
2. Rotate the 500 uL immunoprecipitation reaction for 2 h at 4 C.
3. Place the tube on the magnetic rack for 2 min at 4 C, or until the liquid is clear.
4. Transfer the 500 uL flowthrough to a clean tube without disturbing the retained beads. Keep the flowthrough on ice.

## Wash and Elute the Bead-Bound Protein

1. Add 200 uL ice-cold washing buffer to the retained beads.
2. Resuspend the beads by pipetting 10 times with a pipette set to 160 uL.
3. Rotate for 5 min at 4 C.
4. Place the tube on the magnetic rack for 2 min at 4 C and discard the wash supernatant.
5. Repeat steps 1-4 two additional times for a total of three washes.
6. Add 20 uL denaturing elution buffer and 10 uL premixed 4x LDS sample buffer with reducing agent to the washed beads.
7. Resuspend the beads by pipetting 10 times with a pipette set to 24 uL.
8. Heat for 10 min at 70 C.
9. Place the tube on the magnetic rack for 2 min at 25 C.
10. Transfer the 30 uL eluate to a clean tube while leaving the spent magnetic beads in the original tube.

## Prepare Input, Flowthrough, and Eluate Samples

1. Combine 10 uL input fraction with 10 uL 2x LDS sample buffer containing reducing agent.
2. Combine 10 uL flowthrough with 10 uL 2x LDS sample buffer containing reducing agent.
3. Heat the prepared input and flowthrough samples for 10 min at 70 C.
4. Briefly centrifuge the input, flowthrough, and eluate tubes at 3,000 x g for 5 s at 25 C.

## SDS-PAGE

1. Install a 4-12% Bis-Tris gel in an electrophoresis tank containing 800 mL 1x MOPS running buffer.
2. Load 15 uL prepared input sample into lane 1.
3. Load 15 uL prepared flowthrough sample into lane 2.
4. Load 20 uL eluate into lane 3.
5. Run electrophoresis at 180 V for 50 min at 25 C, or until the dye front reaches the bottom of the gel.

## Transfer Proteins to PVDF Membrane

1. Activate one PVDF membrane in 15 mL methanol for 1 min at 25 C.
2. Remove the methanol and rinse the membrane in 15 mL water for 2 min at 25 C.
3. Equilibrate the membrane and gel separately in 15 mL cold transfer buffer for 10 min.
4. Assemble the transfer stack with the gel facing the negative electrode and the PVDF membrane facing the positive electrode.
5. Transfer proteins at 100 V for 60 min at 4 C.
6. Move the PVDF membrane to a clean membrane-processing tank and rinse it once with 15 mL water.

## Block, Probe, Wash, and Image the Membrane

1. Add 15 mL 5% nonfat milk in TBST and shake the membrane at 60 rpm for 1 h at 25 C.
2. Remove the blocking solution.
3. Add 10 mL rabbit anti-GFP primary antibody diluted 1:1,000 in 5% BSA in TBST.
4. Shake the membrane at 60 rpm for 16 h at 4 C.
5. Remove the primary-antibody solution.
6. Wash the membrane three times with 15 mL TBST for 5 min at 25 C with shaking at 60 rpm, removing the wash buffer after each wash.
7. Add 10 mL IRDye anti-rabbit secondary antibody diluted 1:10,000 in 5% milk in TBST.
8. Incubate for 1 h at 25 C with shaking at 60 rpm while protecting the membrane from light.
9. Remove the secondary-antibody solution.
10. Wash the membrane three times with 15 mL TBST for 5 min at 25 C with shaking at 60 rpm, removing the wash buffer after each wash.
11. Acquire the input, flowthrough, and eluate fluorescence signals with a near-infrared Western blot imager.
12. Record lane identity, sample identity, target-band signal, input signal, flowthrough signal, eluate signal, and imaging QC status in the final readout.
