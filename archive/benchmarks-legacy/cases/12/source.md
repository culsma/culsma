### 2.3 cDNA Amplification

This standalone benchmark case starts with bead-bound template-switched cDNA input material from the preceding template-switch workflow. The captured cDNA is washed and amplified with Template Switch Primer and Read 2-specific primers.

#### Reagents

| Item | Source | Quantity | Handling and storage |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| cDNA Amp Mix | -20C Reagents | 1 | Thaw at room temperature, then place on ice. Mix by inverting 3x. Briefly centrifuge before use. |
| cDNA Amp Primers | -20C Reagents | 1 | Thaw at room temperature, then place on ice. Mix by inverting 3x. Briefly centrifuge before use. |

#### cDNA Amplification Master Mix

Prepare the cDNA Amplification Master Mix in a new 2 mL tube. Mix by pipetting 10x and store on ice.

| Number of sublibraries | cDNA Amp Mix | cDNA Amp Primers | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 60.5 uL | 60.5 uL | 121 uL |
| 16 | 968 uL | 968 uL | 1936 uL |

#### Amplification Procedure

1. Place each tube of bead-bound template-switched cDNA input material on the high position of the magnetic rack.
2. Incubate until the solution clears, about 2 minutes. If beads have settled, pipette mix to resuspend them so they separate appropriately.
3. While still on the magnetic rack, remove and discard the supernatant.
4. While still on the magnetic rack, add 125 uL of Wash Buffer 3 to each tube.
5. Incubate for 1 minute at room temperature.
6. While still on the magnetic rack, remove and discard Wash Buffer 3.
7. Remove the tube(s) from the magnetic rack.
8. Fully resuspend each bead pellet with 100 uL of the Amplification Master Mix.
9. Store on ice.
10. Determine the number of PCR cycles required for cDNA amplification based on input cell or nuclei count and sample type.

| Cells/nuclei in sublibrary | High RNA content, such as cell lines | Low RNA content, such as PBMCs | Nuclei |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 200-1,000 | 11 | 13 | 12 |
| 1,000-2,000 | 9 | 11 | 10 |
| 2,000-6,000 | 7 | 9 | 8 |
| 6,000-12,500 | 6 | 8 | 7 |
| 12,500-25,000 | 4 | 6 | 5 |
| 25,000-62,500 | 3 | 5 | 4 |

11. Place the tube(s) into a thermocycler and run the cDNA Amplification program.

| Run time | Lid temperature | Sample volume |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 50-70 min | 105C | 100 uL |

| Step | Time | Temperature | Cycles |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 3 min | 95C | 1 |
| 2 | 20 sec | 98C | 5 |
| 3 | 45 sec | 65C | 5 |
| 4 | 3 min | 72C | 5 |
| 5 | 20 sec | 98C | Variable, see cycle table |
| 6 | 20 sec | 67C | Variable, see cycle table |
| 7 | 3 min | 72C | Variable, see cycle table |
| 8 | 5 min | 72C | 1 |
| 9 | Hold | 4C | 1 |

If processing sublibraries with different numbers of cells or nuclei, amplify them in separate thermocyclers according to the cycle recommendations above. Annealing steps 3 and 6 have different time and temperature settings.

Amplified cDNA can be stored at 4C for up to 18 hours.
