# 2.4. Post-Amplification Purification

Source: Parse Biosciences, *Evercode WT Mega v3 User Manual*, version 1.5,
November 2024, document UMWT3500, Section 2.4, printed pages 46-47.
[Original manual](https://support.parsebiosciences.com/hc/en-us/article_attachments/31841833600532).

Original section and procedure numbers are retained below. Parenthesized S labels
are benchmark subdivisions; multiple S labels can refer to one original step.
Tables and instructions are formatted for reading, with program choices separated
at the end. Preparation steps outside the numbered S sequence remain included.

Purify the amplified cDNA with a 0.8x SPRI bead cleanup.

## Procedure (original numbering)

**1. (S1)** For each tube of amplified cDNA, gather 400 uL of freshly prepared 85% ethanol.

**2. (S2)** Gather room-temperature SPRI beads, 80 uL per tube of amplified cDNA. Ensure the SPRI beads have been equilibrated to room temperature for at least 30 minutes.

**3. (S3, S4)** Place each tube of amplified cDNA from Section 2.3 on the high position of the Parse Biosciences magnetic rack.

Incubate until the solution clears, about 2 minutes. If beads remain in solution after 2-3 minutes, pipette 3x in the bottom of the PCR tube with a P200 set to 40 uL, then return to the magnet and incubate until the solution clears.

**4. (S5)** While still on the magnetic rack, transfer 90 uL of the supernatant containing the cDNA into a new 0.2 mL tube. Store at room temperature.

**5. (S6, S7)** Vortex the SPRI beads until fully mixed.

Add 72 uL of SPRI beads to each tube with amplified cDNA. This is a 0.8x SPRI bead cleanup.

**6. (S8, S9)** Vortex the tube(s) for 5 seconds.

Briefly centrifuge.

**7. (S10)** Incubate for 5 minutes at room temperature.

**8. (S11, S12)** Place the tube(s) on the high position of the magnetic rack.

Incubate until the solution clears, about 2 minutes.

**9. (S13)** While still on the magnetic rack, remove and discard the supernatant.

**10. (S14)** While still on the magnetic rack, add 180 uL of 85% ethanol to each tube.

**11. (S15)** Incubate for 1 minute at room temperature.

**12. (S16)** While still on the magnetic rack, remove and discard the supernatant.

**13. (S17, S18)** Repeat the ethanol wash once more for a total of 2 washes.

Remove any residual ethanol with a P20.

**14. (S19)** While still on the magnetic rack, air dry the SPRI beads for about 2 minutes. Do not over-dry the beads. Over-drying can substantially reduce yield; cracking of the beads is a sign of over-drying.

**15. (S20, S21)** Remove the tube(s) from the magnetic rack.

Fully resuspend each bead pellet with 25 uL of nuclease-free water.

**16. (S22)** Incubate for 10 minutes at 37C in a thermocycler.

**17. (S23, S24)** Place the tube(s) on the low magnet position of the magnetic rack, so the magnet is closer to the bottom of the 0.2 mL tubes.

Incubate until the solution clears, about 2 minutes. See the manual's "Magnetic Racks and Bead Cleanups" guidelines for an image of the low position.

**18. (S25, S26)** While still on the magnetic rack, transfer 25 uL of the supernatant containing the purified cDNA into new 0.2 mL tube(s).

Store on ice.

**Safe stopping point after step 18 (S27)** Purified amplified cDNA can be stored at 4C for up to 48 hours or at -20C for up to 3 months. Otherwise, proceed immediately to Section 3.

## Benchmark mapping and execution choices (not original instructions)

The program's 100 uL input is based on the preceding Section 2.3 reaction volume;
it is not an extra instruction in Section 2.4. Input composition, material
partition ratios, and unspecified execution settings are benchmark choices.
The continuation to Section 3 above is retained as printed in the manual; this
case ends after purification and does not implement that downstream section.
