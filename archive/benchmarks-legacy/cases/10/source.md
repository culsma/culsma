# 2.1. cDNA Capture

Source: Parse Biosciences, *Evercode WT Mega v3 User Manual*, version 1.5,
November 2024, document UMWT3500, Section 2.1, printed pages 37-40.
[Original manual](https://support.parsebiosciences.com/hc/en-us/article_attachments/31841833600532).

Original section and procedure numbers are retained below. S1-S7 are additional
benchmark stage groupings; each group contains one or more original steps.
Tables and instructions are formatted for reading, with program choices separated
at the end. Preparation steps outside the numbered S sequence remain included.

The barcoded cDNA is captured with streptavidin-coated magnetic beads and washed to remove cellular debris.

#### Preparation

**1.** Fill an ice bucket.
**2.** For each lysate, prepare 400 uL of 85% ethanol with nuclease-free water.
**3.** Equilibrate 80 uL of SPRI beads per lysate to room temperature.
**4.** Gather the following equipment:
   - Magnetic rack for 1.5 mL tubes.
   - Parse Biosciences magnetic rack for 0.2 mL PCR tubes.
   - Vortex with an adapter for 96-well plates.

#### Original step 5: Reagents

| Item | Source | Quantity | Handling and storage |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Streptavidin Beads | 4C Reagents | 1 | Keep at room temperature. |
| Bead Wash Buffer | -20C Reagents | 1 | Thaw at room temperature, then store on ice. Mix by inverting 3x. |
| Wash Buffer 1 | -20C Reagents | 1 | Thaw at room temperature, then store on ice. Mix by inverting 3x. |
| Wash Buffer 2 | -20C Reagents | 1 | Thaw at room temperature, then store on ice. Mix by inverting 3x. |
| Capture Enhancer | -20C Reagents | 1 | Place directly on ice. Briefly centrifuge before use. |
| Binding Buffer | -20C Reagents | 1 | Thaw at room temperature, then store on ice. Mix by inverting 3x. |

## Procedure (original numbering)

### S1. Lysate pretreatment

**6. (S1)** Remove the desired tube(s) of lysate from the thermocycler if continuing directly from Section 1, or from storage at -80C.

**7. (S1)** If previously frozen, incubate the tube(s) in a water bath or thermocycler at 37C for 5 minutes.

Ensure there is no precipitation before proceeding. If precipitation remains, incubate for an additional 5 minutes at 37C.

**8. (S1)** Briefly centrifuge and store at room temperature.

**9. (S1)** Briefly centrifuge Capture Enhancer and gently mix by pipetting 2x with a P20 set to 15 uL.

**10. (S1)** Add 2.5 uL of Capture Enhancer to each tube of lysate.

**11. (S1)** Place the tube(s) into a 96-well PCR tube rack, press to secure, ensure the caps are secured tightly, and place the lid on the rack.

**12. (S1)** Place the rack onto a vortex mixer with a plate adapter. Push to secure.

Vortex the plastic holder on 100% power for 1 minute.

**13. (S1)** Remove the tube(s) from the vortex mixer and briefly centrifuge.

**14. (S1)** Incubate for 10 minutes at room temperature. Proceed immediately to the next step during the incubation. This incubation can be extended by 5 additional minutes, up to a total of 15 minutes, without negatively impacting performance.

### S2. Streptavidin bead washing

**15. (S2)** Vortex Streptavidin Beads until fully mixed.

Add the appropriate volume of Streptavidin Beads to a new 1.5 mL tube:

| Lysates being processed | Streptavidin Beads |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | 44 uL |
| 16 | 704 uL |

**16. (S2)** Place the tube on the magnetic rack for 1.5 mL tubes until the solution clears, about 2 minutes.

**17. (S2)** Remove and discard the supernatant.

**18. (S2)** Remove the tube from the magnetic rack and fully resuspend the bead pellet in the appropriate volume of Bead Wash Buffer. Ensure no beads are stuck to the sides of the 1.5 mL tube.

| Lysates being processed | Bead Wash Buffer |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | 50 uL |
| 16 | 800 uL |

**19. (S2)** Place the tube on the magnetic rack for 1.5 mL tubes until the solution clears, about 2 minutes.

**20. (S2)** Remove and discard the supernatant.

**21. (S2)** Repeat the bead wash twice more for a total of 3 washes.

### S3. Preparation of beads in Binding Buffer

**22. (S3)** Remove the tube from the magnetic rack.

Fully resuspend the pellet in the appropriate volume of Binding Buffer and store at room temperature.

| Lysates being processed | Binding Buffer |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | 55 uL |
| 16 | 880 uL |

### S4. cDNA capture

**23. (S4)** Add 50 uL of Streptavidin Beads in Binding Buffer to each tube of lysate.

**24. (S4)** Place the tube(s) into a 96-well PCR tube rack, press to secure, ensure the caps are secured tightly, and place the lid on the rack.

**25. (S4)** Place the rack onto a vortex mixer with a plate adapter. Push to secure.

Vortex the plastic holder on 100% power for 1 minute.

**26. (S4)** Vortex on 20% power, about 800-1000 RPM, for 30 minutes at room temperature. Check 10 minutes into incubation that the beads are in solution. If settled, increase vortex speed to keep beads in solution.

**27. (S4)** Remove the tube(s) from the vortex mixer.

**28. (S4)** Briefly vortex the tube(s) on a standard vortex adapter.

Briefly centrifuge without letting beads collect at the bottom of the tube(s).

### S5. Magnetic collection of captured cDNA

**29. (S5)** Place the tube(s) on the high position of the magnetic rack, so the magnet is closer to the top of the 0.2 mL tubes. Incubate until the solution clears, about 2 minutes.

Ensure the supernatant is completely clear before proceeding. Discarding beads in the supernatant can reduce transcripts and genes detected per cell. See the manual's "Magnetic Racks and Bead Cleanups" guidelines for representative images.

**30. (S5)** While still on the magnetic rack, remove and discard the supernatant.

### S6. Wash Buffer 1 washes

**31. (S6)** Remove the tube(s) from the magnetic rack.

Fully resuspend each bead pellet with 125 uL Wash Buffer 1.

**32. (S6)** Incubate for 1 minute at room temperature.

**33. (S6)** Return the tube(s) to the high position of the magnetic rack and incubate until the solution clears, about 2 minutes.

**34. (S6)** While still on the magnetic rack, remove and discard the supernatant.

**35. (S6)** Repeat the Wash Buffer 1 wash once more for a total of 2 washes.

### S7. Final resuspension and handoff

**36. (S7)** Remove the tube(s) from the magnetic rack.

Fully resuspend each bead pellet with 125 uL Wash Buffer 2. Save Wash Buffer 2 for optional storage before cDNA amplification.

**37. (S7)** Incubate for 1 minute at room temperature.

**38. (S7)** Proceed immediately to Section 2.2.

## Benchmark mapping and execution choices (not original instructions)

| Benchmark group | Original steps in Section 2.1 |
| --- | --- |
| S1: Lysate pretreatment | 6-14 |
| S2: Streptavidin bead washing | 15-21 |
| S3: Preparation of beads in Binding Buffer | 22 |
| S4: cDNA capture | 23-28 |
| S5: Magnetic collection of captured cDNA | 29-30 |
| S6: Wash Buffer 1 washes | 31-35 |
| S7: Final resuspension and handoff | 36-38 |

Grouping preserves the source instructions, checks, and repetition counts. It does
not change the source requirement to prepare beads during the step 14 incubation.

The standalone case starts at the input boundary of Section 2.1 and applies the
previously frozen input branch. As a benchmark scheduling choice, the program
prepares beads (S2-S3, original steps 15-22) before lysate pretreatment (S1,
original steps 7-14). Original step 22 permits storing prepared beads at room
temperature. The program adds these prepared beads immediately after the
10-minute sample incubation, rather than adding bead-preparation time after it.
The source instead prepares beads during that incubation. S labels identify
source groups, not chronological execution positions.

Explicit room-temperature values, unspecified mixing/spin settings, and material
partition ratios are benchmark choices, not measurements or validated device settings.

### Review decisions applied to the program

| Issue / decision | Current implementation |
| --- | --- |
| Stage fragmentation and ordering | Seven source groups; S2-S3 are prepared before S1, so the source's 10-minute sample incubation is followed immediately by bead addition. |
| Three S1 brief centrifugations (original 8, 9, 13) | Standalone `sep` in the same tube, with no portion withdrawn or discarded; authored settings are 3000g, 5s, 25C. Proprietary Capture Enhancer has explicit liquid-phase routing. |
| Missing S4 brief centrifugation (original 28) | A 5s vortex on the standard adapter precedes standalone `sep` at 100g for 8s at 25C. Explicit fates leave CE, LYSATE1 and STREP_BEADS in the supernatant portion; this models the source requirement not to pellet beads. These are authored settings, not experimentally tested settings. |
| Precipitation check (original 7) | `LysatePrecipitationCheck` readout gates processing. If precipitation is present, heat at 37C for five additional minutes and recheck; persistent precipitation stops dependent operations. |
| Bead suspension check (original 26) | `CaptureBeadSuspensionCheck` at minute 10. If suspended, continue 20 minutes at 800rpm. Otherwise increase to 1000rpm, recheck after one minute, then continue for 19 minutes only if suspended. Successful branches both total 30 minutes of mixing. The retry interval and exact speeds are author choices within the stated workflow. |
| Clear supernatant before removal (original 16-20, 29-34) | All seven magnetic removals use `MagneticSupernatantClarity`. After the initial two-minute separation, uncleared samples stay on the magnet for an authored one-minute retry. Failed rechecks leave contents undiscarded and stop dependent operations for intervention. The source does not specify this retry limit. |
| Reagent preparation (original 5) | Four buffer inversions, three cycles each, are executable at the modeled ice temperature. A preparation confirmation covers room-temperature thawing, storage on ice, Capture Enhancer on ice and beads at room temperature. This standalone input boundary assumes supplied thawed reagents; it does not invent thaw durations. Ethanol and SPRI preparation belongs to the later purification boundary and is not consumed in this case. |
| Gentle enhancer mixing and equipment handling | Two 15uL self-transfers carry `constraint(gentle)`. Setup readouts gate the dependent actions: secured caps/rack/lid/adapter, 100% and 20% vortex settings, the standard adapter, and the correct magnetic rack/high position. These confirmations do not claim automatic hardware control. |
| Existing cDNA capture semantics | Preserve explicit `bound / flowthrough` fates. LYSATE1 is retained with the beads; CE goes to flowthrough. The earlier suggestion that capture was missing is withdrawn. No new separation operation or inferred yield is introduced. |
| Material quantities and tube capacity | Preserve the existing author inputs and 250uL computational capacity. The source calls for 0.2mL tubes. Because the current input represents 55uL lysate as DNA and 44uL bead suspension as beads, modeled final volume remains about 202.22uL. This is a known representation limitation, not a measured liquid volume or evidence of missing capture; changing fates or silently setting capacity to 200uL would not resolve it. |
| Lysate state and output boundary | The input state is the literal string `"lysate"`. This remains an independent Section 2.1 case; it does not run Section 2.2. The material report retains its output and the workflow status distinguishes completed capture from partial material after a failed check. |

### Check inputs and execution evidence

The `data_schema` declarations define recorded fields; readouts and `if` conditions
implement the continuation rules. The workflow status records `can_continue`,
`capture_complete`, and `needs_attention`. A failed check stops dependent
operations and sets attention status; it is not a simulated successful capture.

The default invocation uses deterministic scenario inputs, not experimental
observations. Set `use_fixture_inputs = false` to consume driver/operator-provided
readout results without overwriting them. External result acquisition is the
responsibility of the driver; this program does not implement a sensor or an
interactive acquisition UI.

| Scenario parameter | Meaning / default |
| --- | --- |
| `fixture_reagents_ready` | Preparation confirmed; `true`. |
| `fixture_equipment_ready` | Equipment confirmations pass; `true`. |
| `fixture_precipitate_present`, `fixture_precipitate_after_reheat` | Precipitation before/after extra heating; both `false`. |
| `fixture_beads_suspended`, `fixture_beads_suspended_after_speedup` | Suspension before/after increased speed; both `true`. |
| `fixture_unclear_check` | First unclear magnetic check, numbered 1-7; `0` means all clear initially. Checks 1-4 cover bead preparation, 5 captures cDNA, and 6-7 cover WB1 washes. |
| `fixture_clear_after_retry` | Magnetic retry clears the sample; `true`. |

Regression coverage in `tests/test_case10_brief_centrifugation.py` exercises both
human and robot projections, material preservation during all four spins,
precipitation and suspension recovery/failure, each of the seven clarity gates,
unconfirmed setup, and externally supplied readout results. This verifies
program behavior, not experimental performance.
