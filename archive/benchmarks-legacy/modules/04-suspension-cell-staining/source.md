# Module 04: Suspension-Cell Antibody Staining

## Steps

1. Start with a prepared PBMC suspension in flow-cytometry staining buffer and adjust it to 1 x 10^7 cells/mL.
2. Assign six 5 mL staining tubes: one fully stained sample, one unstained control, one isotype-control panel, and three single-antibody controls corresponding to the three antibody fluorophores in the full panel.
3. Aliquot 100 uL PBMC suspension, corresponding to 1 x 10^6 cells, into each of the six staining tubes.
4. Add 2 mL staining buffer to each tube.
5. Centrifuge all tubes at 300 x g for 5 min at 4 C, then remove and discard each supernatant without disturbing the cell pellet.
6. Add 65 uL staining buffer to each cell pellet and pipette 10 times using 52 uL per stroke to fully resuspend the cells.
7. Add 5 uL human Fc receptor blocking reagent to each tube.
8. Pipette each 70 uL suspension 10 times using 56 uL per stroke, then incubate for 10 min on ice.
9. Use anti-CD45-FITC, anti-CD3-PE, and anti-CD4-Cy5 stocks at 5 mg/mL each. Prepare 30 uL full staining panel containing 5 uL of each antibody stock, 5 uL viability dye, and 10 uL staining buffer.
10. Use FITC-, PE-, and Cy5-matched isotype-control antibody stocks at 5 mg/mL. Prepare 30 uL isotype-control panel containing 5 uL of each of the three isotype-control stocks, 5 uL viability dye, and 10 uL staining buffer.
11. For each of the three single-antibody controls, prepare 30 uL staining reagent containing 5 uL of the assigned 5 mg/mL antibody stock and 25 uL staining buffer.
12. Prepare 30 uL unstained-control reagent containing staining buffer only.
13. Add the complete 30 uL full staining panel to the fully stained sample tube.
14. Add the complete 30 uL isotype-control panel to the isotype-control tube.
15. Add each complete 30 uL single-antibody reagent to its assigned single-antibody control tube.
16. Add 30 uL staining buffer to the unstained-control tube.
17. Pipette each 100 uL staining suspension 10 times using 80 uL per stroke, then incubate for 30 min on ice protected from light.
18. Add 2 mL staining buffer to each tube.
19. Centrifuge all tubes at 300 x g for 5 min at 4 C, then remove and discard each supernatant without disturbing the cell pellet.
20. Add 2 mL fresh staining buffer to each tube.
21. Pipette each 2 mL cell suspension 10 times using 1.6 mL per stroke to fully resuspend the cells.
22. Centrifuge all tubes at 300 x g for 5 min at 4 C, then remove and discard each supernatant without disturbing the cell pellet.
23. Prepare 1.8 mL 1x fixation buffer for the six samples by combining 180 uL 10x fixation-buffer stock with 1.62 mL PBS. Add 300 uL 1x fixation buffer to each cell pellet.
24. Pipette each 300 uL suspension 10 times using 240 uL per stroke, then incubate for 10 min at 22 C protected from light.
25. Add 2 mL staining buffer to each tube.
26. Centrifuge all tubes at 300 x g for 5 min at 4 C, then remove and discard each supernatant without disturbing the cell pellet.
27. Add 500 uL staining buffer to each fixed cell pellet and pipette 10 times using 400 uL per stroke to fully resuspend the cells.
28. Keep all six stained cell suspensions at 4 C protected from light for no longer than 24 h before transfer to a downstream acquisition module.
29. Return the fully stained sample, unstained control, isotype-control panel, and three single-antibody controls as six separate stained cell suspensions while preserving their sample and control identities.
