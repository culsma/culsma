# Module 03: Adherent-Cell Lysate Preparation

## Steps

1. Use one well of a 6-well plate containing 5 x 10^6 adherent mammalian cells at the protein-harvest point.
2. Aspirate and discard the culture medium without disturbing the adherent cell monolayer.
3. Wash the adherent cell monolayer twice with 1 mL cold 1x PBS per wash. For each wash, add the PBS, rinse the complete monolayer, and aspirate and discard the PBS without disturbing the cells.
4. Prepare 300 uL cold complete RIPA lysis buffer by combining 297 uL RIPA buffer with 3 uL 100x protease and phosphatase inhibitor cocktail, giving a final inhibitor concentration of 1x. Add the complete 300 uL mixture directly to the washed cell monolayer.
5. Incubate the cells in the lysis buffer on ice for 20 min.
6. Transfer the complete crude lysate from the well to a clean 1.5 mL microcentrifuge tube.
7. Mix the crude lysate by pipetting 10 times with the pipette set to 240 uL.
8. Centrifuge the crude lysate at 12,000 x g for 10 min at 4 C.
9. Transfer the clarified supernatant to a clean 1.5 mL microcentrifuge tube without disturbing the insoluble pellet, and discard the pellet.
10. Return the clarified protein lysate for downstream protein quantification, purification, or detection.
